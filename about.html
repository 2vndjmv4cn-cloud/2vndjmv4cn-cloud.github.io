About

D. Rose Free Calculators is a small set of free tools made by D. Rose Studios, which also makes budget and planning spreadsheets.

We built these calculators to be quick, private and easy to understand. Each one shows the formula it uses and a worked example, so you can check the math yourself.

How we keep them accurate

Every calculator is tested against known results before it goes live, for example standard loan payment and compound interest figures. If you spot a mistake, please tell us through the contact page and we'll fix it.

How the site is paid for

Some pages link to spreadsheets in our own shop, and those links are labeled. The calculators are free either way. If we add advertising in the future, we'll update the privacy policy first.

Content is for education and planning, not financial, tax or legal advice.
