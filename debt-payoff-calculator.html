Snowball or avalanche?

Both methods pay the minimum on every debt and put all extra money on one debt at a time. When that debt is gone, its payment rolls onto the next one. The only difference is the order:

  • Snowball: smallest balance first. You clear whole debts sooner, which many people find motivating.
  • Avalanche: highest interest rate first. This usually costs less interest overall.

How this calculator works

Each month it adds interest to every debt (APR ÷ 12), pays every minimum, then puts the extra money, plus the minimums of any debts already paid off, on the target debt. Your total monthly payment stays the same until everything is paid.

Questions

Which method is better?

Avalanche saves the most money. Snowball can be easier to stick with. The best plan is the one you'll actually follow.

What about 0% balance transfers?

This tool doesn't model promotional rates or transfer fees. If you have a 0% period, enter 0 as the APR and remember the rate will change when the promotion ends.
