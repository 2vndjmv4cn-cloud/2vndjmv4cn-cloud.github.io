Savings Goal Calculator

Find out how much to save per month, week or day to reach your goal by a target date.

This calculator is for education and planning only. It is not financial advice. Real loans, cards and accounts can have fees, rate changes and rules this tool doesn't include.

How it works

Take your goal, subtract what you already have (plus any interest it will earn), and spread the rest over the months you have. With no interest that's simply:

(Goal − Saved) ÷ Months

If you enter an interest rate, the calculator also counts the interest your deposits earn along the way, so the monthly amount comes out a little lower. Amounts are rounded up to the next cent so you don't fall short.

Example

To turn $500 into $5,000 in 12 months with no interest, you need $4,500 more, which is $375 a month: about $86.54 a week or $12.33 a day.

Tips for sticking to it

  • Set up an automatic transfer on payday so the money moves before you can spend it.
  • Keep the savings in a separate account from your spending money.
  • If the monthly number is too high, add a few months rather than giving up on the goal.

Questions

What's a good first savings goal?

Many people start with a small emergency fund, such as $500 to $1,000, then build toward three to six months of essential expenses.

Should I save or pay off debt first?

A common approach is a small emergency fund first, then extra payments on high-interest debt, then larger savings goals.
