Hourly to Salary Calculator

Convert an hourly wage into yearly, monthly, biweekly and weekly pay before taxes.

This calculator is for education and planning only. It is not financial advice. Real loans, cards and accounts can have fees, rate changes and rules this tool doesn't include.

The quick formula

Yearly pay = hourly rate × hours per week × paid weeks per year

A full-time year is usually counted as 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, which is 2,080 hours. A quick shortcut: double the hourly rate and add three zeros. $20 an hour is about $40,000 a year (the exact figure is $41,600).

Example

At $20 an hour, 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year: $41,600 a year, $3,466.67 a month, $1,600 every two weeks and $800 a week.

Before taxes

These are gross amounts. Take-home pay is lower after income tax, Social Security, Medicare and any benefits or retirement contributions. If you have unpaid time off, lower the weeks per year.

Questions

Why does monthly pay differ from 4 weekly paychecks?

Most months are a little longer than four weeks. A year has 52 weeks, so monthly pay is the yearly amount divided by 12, which is about 4.33 weeks.

How many paychecks is biweekly?

Paid every two weeks means 26 paychecks a year, so two months each year have three paydays.
