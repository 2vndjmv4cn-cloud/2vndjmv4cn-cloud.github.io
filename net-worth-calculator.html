What net worth means

Net worth = everything you own − everything you owe. It's a single number that shows your overall financial position, and the trend over time matters more than the number on any one day.

Example

Someone with $15,000 in savings and a $200,000 home, who owes $150,000 on the mortgage and $5,000 on a car, has a net worth of $60,000.

Tips

  • Use what things would actually sell for today, not what you paid.
  • Check it every three to six months. Paying down debt raises your net worth just like saving does.

Questions

Is my information saved?

No. The numbers stay in your browser and disappear when you leave the page.

What is a debt-to-asset ratio?

Total debts divided by total assets. Lower is generally stronger; above 100% means you owe more than you own.
