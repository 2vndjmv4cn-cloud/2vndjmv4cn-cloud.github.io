Split Costs Calculator

Enter what each person paid and get the fewest payments to settle up.

Enter what each person paid in total. Example names are filled in.

This calculator is for education and planning only. It is not financial advice. Real loans, cards and accounts can have fees, rate changes and rules this tool doesn't include.

How it settles up

The calculator adds up everything that was paid and divides it evenly. Anyone who paid more than their share gets money back; anyone who paid less pays the difference. It then matches people up so you need as few payments as possible.

Example

Sam paid $240, Alex paid $60 and Jordan paid nothing. The total is $300, or $100 each. Jordan pays Sam $100 and Alex pays Sam $40, and everyone is even.

Good for

  • Group trips and vacation rentals
  • Shared dinners and groceries
  • Roommate bills

Amounts are split to the cent; if a total doesn't divide evenly, the extra cent goes to one person.

Questions

Can it split unevenly?

Not yet. It splits everything equally. For uneven splits, enter only the shared costs.

Is anything saved?

No. Names and amounts stay in your browser.
