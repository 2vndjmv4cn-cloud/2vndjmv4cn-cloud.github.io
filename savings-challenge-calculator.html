Savings Challenge Calculator

See how much you'll save with the 52 week, 100 envelope, 26 paycheck and penny challenges.

This calculator is for education and planning only. It is not financial advice. Real loans, cards and accounts can have fees, rate changes and rules this tool doesn't include.

How the totals work

These challenges save a little more each time. Adding up 1 + 2 + 3 + ... + n gives n × (n + 1) ÷ 2, then you multiply by the starting amount.

  • 52 week challenge: $1 in week 1 up to $52 in week 52 = $1,378.
  • 100 envelope challenge: envelopes worth $1 to $100 = $5,050.
  • 26 paycheck challenge: $10, $20 ... $260 = $3,510.
  • Penny a day: 1 cent up to $3.65 = $667.95.

Make it easier

Do it in reverse: start with the biggest amounts while you're motivated and finish with the small ones near the holidays. Or pick envelopes at random so big and small amounts mix.

Questions

Can I start with $2 or $5 instead of $1?

Yes. Change the starting amount and every step scales. A $5 start on the 52 week challenge saves $6,890.

Where should I keep the money?

Cash envelopes work, but a separate savings account keeps it safe and may earn a little interest.
